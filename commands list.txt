SentriX command reference

Telegram Bot Menu (only these four commands)
- /start - Open the SentriX welcome screen
- /help - Open the interactive Help Center
- /ping - Check bot status
- /settings - Open the inline group settings dashboard

All other commands are available from /help category buttons.

Bot and information
- /about
- /features
- /id [user]
- /info
- /stats
- /admins
- /adminlist
- /report [reason]

Administration and moderation
- /promote <user>
- /demote <user>
- /ban <user> [duration] [reason]
- /unban <user>
- /kick <user> [reason]
- /kickme
- /mute <user> [duration] [reason]
- /unmute <user>
- /warn <user> [reason]
- /unwarn <user>
- /warnings [user]
- /purge [count]
- /pin and /unpin
- /case <case_id>
- /mod and /modinfo
- /auth, /unauth, /grant, /revoke
- /freeze, /unfreeze, /badge
- /protect, /unprotect, /protected
- /warnconfig and /warnmode

Protection
- /antispam [on|off]
- /antiraid [on|off]
- /captcha [on|off]
- /setflood <messages> [seconds]
- /lock <type> and /unlock <type>
- /locktype, /locktypes, /locktypelist, /locklist
- /lockbot, /locklink, /unlockbot, /unlocklink
- /addblocklist, /blocklist, /deleteblocklist, /blocklists, /blocklistmode
- /bot on|off

Welcome and rules
- /welcome and /setwelcome <text>
- /goodbye and /setgoodbye <text>
- /rules and /setrules <text>
- /setup, /reset, /language

Filters and notes
- /filter <keyword> <response>
- /filters
- /stop <keyword>
- /stopall
- /save <name> <text>
- /get <name>
- /notes
- /clear <name>
- /custom <name> <response>
- /customcommands
- /delcustom <name>

Logging and connections
- /setlog <channel_id> or forward a log-channel message
- /unsetlog
- /logchannel
- /logsettings
- /connect <chat_id>
- /connections
- /connection
- /disconnect [chat_id|all]
- /allowconnections on|off
- /broadcast

Games and appeals
- /ttt
- /tttleaderboard
- /tttmystats
- /tttend
- /appeal <case_id> <message>

Legacy h-prefixed commands are retired and are no longer registered or accepted.
Use /help to browse syntax, permissions, examples, notes, and related commands.
